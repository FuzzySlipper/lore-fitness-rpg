## Premise

The Inquisition has just claimed Skyhold. Corypheus is alive, the Venatori and
the Red Templars move openly, and the Grey Wardens have gone quiet in the
Western Approach. The season ends when the Inquisition is ready to march on
Adamant: allies secured, supply lines safe and a Venatori magister's plan to
break that march defeated. The march itself is season two.

## Acts

1. **The Mountain Holds.** Skyhold becomes a working fortress. Recruits arrive,
   supplies run short, and a tampered supply ledger points to a Venatori
   smuggling ring moving red lyrium through the Frostback passes. Missions in
   the Hinterlands and on the Storm Coast. Act climax: the ring's courier
   network is broken and its paymaster named: Magister Aurelian Tharsis.
2. **Red Lyrium Roads.** The red lyrium comes from a quarry-fortress held by
   Knight-Captain Ilse Varn of the Red Templars. Companion arcs deepen; the
   Inquisitor chooses allies and routes (Fallow Mire, Exalted Plains, Emerald
   Graves). Act climax: the quarry-fortress falls or is starved out, and Varn's
   fate is decided.
3. **Before Adamant.** Tharsis, cut off, gambles on a demon-backed strike at the
   Inquisition's staging camps in the Western Approach. Leliana's network,
   Josephine's alliances and Cullen's troops are tested together. Season
   finale: Tharsis is defeated at a ruined Tevinter watchtower, and the
   Inquisition's banners are raised for the march on Adamant.

## How the story moves

The fiction advances by sessions and chosen missions, never by real-world
days. Activity gives the Inquisitor level, supplies, training and second
chances; it never gates the story. A missed week means Skyhold kept going
without fuss. A return is welcomed. Recovery days fit operations, companion
conversations and war-table planning.

## Original characters

- **Magister Aurelian Tharsis**: a Venatori magister, polished and patient, who
  thinks of the Inquisition as an accounting error. Season antagonist.
- **Knight-Captain Ilse Varn**: once a templar of real faith, now holding a
  red lyrium quarry for Corypheus. Act two antagonist; may be saved, killed or
  left to the lyrium depending on the Inquisitor's choices.
- **Quartermaster Hadley Brisk**: Skyhold's harried quartermaster, who keeps the
  famous tampered ledger and the Inquisition's supplies honest.
