## Order

The Inquisitor commits supplies and labour to Cullen's staging camps in the Western Approach, with tents, horse lines, fire breaks and guard rotas.

## Report

Cullen reports that the camps can hold the army, and that fire breaks have been dug between the tent lines. Guard rotas run in shifts. Scouts have seen men in the scrub at night who never came to the camp, and Cullen has doubled the watch on the ridge.

## Unlocks

Opens a3-staging-camps. Fire breaks and water barrels are ready in a3-camp-raid. Reinforcements from the camps can reach the stair in a3-watchtower-gate. Scene hook: a camp cook asks the Inquisitor to taste the stew, and a recruit quietly confesses a fear of fire.
