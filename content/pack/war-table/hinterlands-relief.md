## Order

The Inquisitor sends Josephine's carts to the refugee camp at the crossroads, loaded with clean water, lanterns, blankets and seed for the spring.

## Report

Josephine reports that the carts reached the crossroads before nightfall. The water barrels are set in a ring around the camp, and the lanterns mark the safe paths between the tents. The refugee count has risen by a dozen families, and a chapel elder asks whether the Inquisition will stay through the winter.

## Unlocks

Opens a1-hinterlands-refugees. Lantern lines are available to the party in a1-hinterlands-rift. Scene hook: the chapel elder offers the Inquisitor a list of families who need work, and one name on it belongs to a former soldier.
