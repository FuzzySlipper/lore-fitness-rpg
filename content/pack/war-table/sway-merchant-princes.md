## Order

The Inquisitor authorises Josephine to court the merchant princes whose coin has kept the Inquisition's campaigns alive, and to share what the Inquisition knows about Tharsis.

## Report

Josephine reports that two princes have pledged their coin to the march. A third refuses to commit until the Inquisition names the magister responsible for the red lyrium trade. Once the name Tharsis is spoken in the council, the refusing prince asks for a private meeting.

## Unlocks

Opens a3-merchant-princes. Pledged coin is available for supplies in a3-watchtower-assault. Scene hook: the refusing prince offers a ship and a list of ports, but only if the Inquisitor attends in person.
