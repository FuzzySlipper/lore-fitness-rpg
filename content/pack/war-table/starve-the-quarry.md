## Order

The Inquisitor orders Cullen's troops and Josephine's quartermasters to cut the supply road to Redcrag and hold it until the quarry's stores run low.

## Report

Cullen reports that the supply road is closed at two points. Red Templar stores are low, and a lyrium wagon has been seen leaving the quarry rather than entering. The garrison's rations have been cut, and some templars have begun to grumble openly about their pay.

## Unlocks

Opens a2-quarry-siege. The garrison is thin in a2-quarry-gates and the behemoth's crew is short in a2-quarry-heart. Scene hook: a Red Templar messenger arrives under a white cloth and asks for terms on behalf of Ilse Varn.
