## Order

The Inquisitor commits Leliana's quiet scouts to the high Frostback passes, to map the switchbacks, the shelters and any traffic on the old mule trails.

## Report

Leliana reports that the scouts mapped a narrow cut between two boulders where riders must pass single file. They found a shelter with fresh boot prints, a mule trail that avoids the main road, and a Venatori seal dropped in the snow. The ice on the cut is heavy and the scouts recommend caution.

## Unlocks

Opens a1-frostback-pass. Makes the flanking route available to the party in a1-frostback-smugglers. Scene hook: a scout returns with a frostbitten hand and asks whether the Inquisition will send a healer back up the pass.
