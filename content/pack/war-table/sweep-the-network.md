## Order

The Inquisitor sets Leliana's agents to sweep the courier network for hidden cells, and to learn any passwords and routes still in use.

## Report

Leliana reports that three hidden cells were found along the courier road. One cell held a password that opens the watchtower's gate. Another left a side postern marked on a map, and the third cell has gone silent since dawn.

## Unlocks

Opens a3-turned-agent. The password and postern are known to the party in a3-watchtower-gate. The rift's anchor is marked on the map in a3-tharsis. Scene hook: an agent from the silent cell asks Leliana for a second chance.
