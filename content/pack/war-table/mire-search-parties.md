## Order

The Inquisitor sends Cullen's trained search parties along the causeways of the Fallow Mire to find the missing scouts and to mark safe ground.

## Report

Cullen reports that the parties found a drowned farmhouse, a line of stakes marking the causeway, and two of the scouts' packs. The grey fog moves with the wind and muffles voices, so the parties now walk in pairs with a rope between them. No one has yet found the scouts themselves.

## Unlocks

Opens a2-missing-scouts. Rope lines and stake markers are available in a2-mire-despair. Scene hook: a party member hears a voice in the reeds that sounds like a familiar name, and asks whether the Inquisition will go back in alone.
