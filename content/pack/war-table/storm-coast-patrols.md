## Order

The Inquisitor asks Cullen to train patrols along the cliff path of the Storm Coast, with signal fires, rope and a shared watch rota.

## Report

Cullen reports that two patrol posts now stand on the cliffs, and the signal fires have been tested in the rain. The patrols have found a cove below the cliffs where wet tracks lead down to the shingle. The patrol leaders say the tide turns faster than the charts suggest.

## Unlocks

Opens a1-storm-coast-chargers. Signal fires and rope are available to the party in a1-storm-coast-cove. Scene hook: a patrol leader asks Iron Bull to teach the new recruits how to read the tide.
