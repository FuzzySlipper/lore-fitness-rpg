## Order

The Inquisitor asks Josephine to have every crate, sack and barrel in Skyhold's stores counted against the quartermaster's ledger, line by line.

## Report

Josephine reports that the count is complete. Most entries were honest slips. One entry bears a Tevinter seal that no quartermaster at Skyhold recognises, and the tally for the Frostback shipments is short by a crate. Hadley Brisk has corrected the ledger and asks that the forged entry be kept for the Inquisitor to inspect.

## Unlocks

Opens the Frostback thread: the forged seal points toward the smuggling route in a1-frostback-pass. Scene hook: Hadley Brisk shows the Inquisitor the corrected ledger and the seal, and asks what should be done with it. Supply counts from this audit are used when Skyhold's stores are sent on a mission.
