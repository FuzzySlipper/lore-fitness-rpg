## Order

The Inquisitor commits time and example to Cullen's training schedule for the
Skyhold garrison.

Cullen reports that the night watch now runs in pairs, the gate crews know
their signals, and two recruits who could not hold a shield last week now can.

## Unlocks

Garrison soldiers can be called into fights at Skyhold (see
a1-lower-gate-ambush). Opens a training-yard scene in which a recruit asks the
Inquisitor for advice.
