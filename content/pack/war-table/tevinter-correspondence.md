## Order

The Inquisitor authorises Josephine to write quietly to old Tevinter contacts, asking about shipments, seals and the names of any magisters who travel the Western Approach.

## Report

Josephine reports that a reply has arrived in an unfamiliar hand. It names a magister's office and mentions a shipment routed through the Frostback passes. The seal pressed on the reply matches the forged seal in the Skyhold ledger.

## Unlocks

Opens a2-dorian-letters, where Dorian reads the reply and recognises the office. Scene hook: a second letter arrives addressed to the Inquisitor by name, and asks for a meeting that nobody at Skyhold has arranged.
