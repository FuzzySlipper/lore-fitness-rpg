## Order

The Inquisitor authorises Josephine to host a feast in Skyhold's great hall for the garrison, the scouts and the companions who have carried the campaign so far.

## Report

Josephine reports that the hall was full, the kitchens ran out of bread by midnight, and the recruits sang until the cooks sent them home. Several garrison soldiers asked to be posted to the next patrol together. The night was quiet, and nobody was sent for at the gate.

## Unlocks

Opens rec-tavern-night for the companions who attended. Scene hook: a recruit asks the Inquisitor for a seat at the next war table, and Josephine quietly agrees to make room.
