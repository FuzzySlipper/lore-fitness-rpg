## Order

The Inquisitor commits stone, timber and masons to Cullen's plan for the walls, the gatehouse and the lower gate of Skyhold.

## Report

Cullen reports that a new portcullis hangs in the lower gate, the walls have been shored up in two places, and the old counterweight chain has been replaced. The masons have also built a ramp behind the gatehouse, so reinforcements can reach the wall walk quickly. Cullen notes the work will need inspecting after the next storm.
