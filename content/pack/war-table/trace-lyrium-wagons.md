## Order

The Inquisitor commits Leliana's network to follow the lyrium wagons from the Frostback passes toward the quarry road, noting every stop, driver and purse.

## Report

Leliana reports that several wagons carry double floors, and the drivers are paid from one purse at each stop. The route runs across the Exalted Plains along the old road. Two drivers were willing to talk, and one of them gave a name that matches a templar in the quarry's service.

## Unlocks

Opens a2-red-road. The false floors and wagon routes are known to the party in a2-red-road-convoy. Scene hook: a driver who talked is offered a new life by the Inquisition, and asks for a guarantee that his family will be safe.
