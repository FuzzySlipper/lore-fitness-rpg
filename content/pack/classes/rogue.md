## At the training yard

Paired daggers up close and a longbow from the treeline: the rogue strikes where
the enemy is not looking. Dexterity and Cunning carry the build; the talents are
Long Shot, Into the Shadows, Twin Fangs and Flurry of a Thousand Cuts.
