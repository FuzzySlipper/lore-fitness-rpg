## How the world sees them

A Qunari Inquisitor here is Vashoth: a Qunari born outside the Qun, who has
left or never accepted its order. Most people in Thedas have only heard of the
Qunari as invaders and foes, and are wary of a horned giant at the head of a
holy army. Chantry clergy and soldiers who remember Kirkwall's Qunari whisper
about spies. A Qunari Inquisitor will draw stares whatever they do, and is
held to a harsher accounting for it.

## Companion reactions

- solas: Treats the Inquisitor as an outsider among outsiders and is curious about how they were raised.
- sera: Makes bad horn jokes and then fights anyone else who does.
- cassandra: Judges them by conduct and trusts them for it; still asks where they stand on the Chantry.
- cullen: Keeps professional distance until trust is earned, then drops it.
- leliana: Notes the political challenge plainly and arranges appearances to match.
- dorian: Delighted and curious, with questions he decides not to ask yet.
- iron-bull: Most affected of anyone; watches the Inquisitor as a Qunari who left the Qun and weighs what it means for his own oath.
- blackwall: Treats them like anyone else and gets on with the work.

## Address and titles

"Inquisitor." Soldiers may use "the Qunari" behind their back; correct it
without a lecture. Iron Bull may use "Vashoth" with both respect and curiosity.
