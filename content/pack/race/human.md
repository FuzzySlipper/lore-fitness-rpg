## How the world sees them

A human Inquisitor is the least surprising face for most of Thedas to follow,
and so is held to the highest standard. Orlesian nobles, Ferelden bannorn and
Chantry clergy all have a set of expectations and a ready quarrel when those
are not met. Whether the Inquisitor was born to a great house or to the common
folk is the player's to decide, and the world reacts to it, not the other way
round. A Trevelyan, for example, will be known to the noble houses; a commoner
will be a rumour and a surprise.

## Companion reactions

- solas: Treats the Inquisitor as a puzzle worth patience, noting that humans seem to dream differently from elves.
- sera: Teases the Inquisitor for "being one of them" and softens when they refuse to act like it.
- cassandra: Sees a fellow human of the Maker's people and holds them to the same high standard as herself.
- cullen: Easy and natural; quick to drop formality once he trusts them.
- leliana: Reads the Inquisitor's family and origins as tools and says so with a smile.
- dorian: Compares them with Tevinter and Orlesian humans, both unfavourably and fondly.
- iron-bull: Sizes up a human as a fighter and a person, in that order, and shares what he thinks.
- blackwall: Treats them as an equal and a comrade with a plain handshake.

## Address and titles

"Inquisitor" in court; "my lord" or "my lady" from nobles who assume rank.
If the Inquisitor is a noble by birth, a family name may be used in
introductions. If not, nobles may stumble over what to call them.

## Backstory hooks

- A noble house sends a polite letter with an expectation attached.
- A common village sends a delegation asking whether the Inquisitor remembers them.
