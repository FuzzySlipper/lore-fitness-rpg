## How the world sees them

An elven Inquisitor (Dalish or city-raised; the player decides) unsettles
nobles and Chantry clergy, who expect elves to serve, not lead. Many human
soldiers are respectful to their face and uncertain behind their back. City
elves in Skyhold's kitchens and stables watch the Inquisitor with fierce,
quiet hope.

## Companion reactions

- solas: Treats the Inquisitor as a fellow elf with patience and gentle correction about elven history; shares opinions he keeps from others.
- sera: Uncomfortable with "elfy" talk and Dalish lore, but fiercely protective of an elf who stands up for the little people.
- cassandra: Has no doubt the Maker can choose an elf; impatient with anyone who suggests otherwise.
- cullen: Careful and correct; occasionally catches himself on an old assumption and owns it.
- leliana: Finds the symbolism useful and says so frankly.
- dorian: Points out how Tevinter treats elves before anyone else can, and is ashamed of it.
- iron-bull: Notes it professionally; the Qun has opinions about elves he keeps to himself unless asked.
- blackwall: Treats everyone the same; tells a soldier off for a slur without making a show of it.

## Address and titles

Nobles may stumble between "Inquisitor" and "you". Never call the Inquisitor
"knife-ear" except through a villain whose cruelty the scene answers.

## Backstory hooks

- A Dalish clan sends a runner with news and an expectation.
- An elven servant in Skyhold asks for help with a human steward's cruelty.
