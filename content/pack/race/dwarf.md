## How the world sees them

A dwarven Inquisitor is a curiosity above ground and a quiet scandal below
it. Surface dwarves are rare and mostly regarded as merchants, smiths or
traders and tellers of tall tales; a leader of the Inquisition is an astonishment.
Many people assume a dwarf cannot wield magic, since dwarves cannot dream or
touch the Fade, and are baffled by the Anchor. Whether the Inquisitor is a
surfacer, a Deep Roads exile or a casteless runaway is the player's to decide.

## Companion reactions

- solas: Fascinated and visibly unsettled; a dwarf cannot dream, and he asks careful questions about how the Anchor behaves.
- sera: Cheers a "little folk" Inquisitor and makes dwarf jokes that are affectionate, not unkind.
- cassandra: Respects a dwarf's craft and candour, and mentions Varric before she can stop herself.
- cullen: Careful and curious, and a little amused by the room's height difference.
- leliana: Notes that few in Orlais have ever met a dwarf, and plans accordingly.
- dorian: Is delighted to be the tallest person at a dinner, and says so.
- iron-bull: Grins and says the Qun has views on dwarves, then admits the Chargers have a few.
- blackwall: Treats the Inquisitor as a veteran, knowing what dwarves face in the Deep Roads.

## Address and titles

"Inquisitor," always; some nobles ask whether "Lord" or "Lady" applies and
are corrected. Varric may use a nickname.

## Backstory hooks

- A stonemason in the Hinterlands recognises the Inquisitor's family name.
- A surface dwarf merchant asks for a favour that touches on Orzammar's politics.
