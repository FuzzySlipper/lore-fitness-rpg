## Setup

The gate of a ruined Tevinter watchtower stands above a collapsed stair, its iron bar still bolted from the inside. Two Venatori gladiators hold the gate, and a horror waits in the archway with its lyrium-wrapped arms folded. The stones are worn smooth by centuries of wind, and carved letters in the lintel have half worn away.

## Twist

The horror tears the iron bar from its brackets, and the gate starts to swing. The gladiators rush to close it, and the party must choose whether to fight through the arch or hold the gate open long enough for the rest of the line to come up the stair.

## Preparation pays off

If the Inquisition's networks were swept (project sweep-the-network), the party knows the gate's password and the side postern that leads into the tower's lower hall. That shortens the fight and gives the party a second route. If the camps in the Approach are held (project western-approach-camps), reinforcements can reach the stair before the gladiators regroup.

## Aftermath

The gate falls or closes, and the tower's lower hall is claimed by the Inquisition. Any Venatori papers found in the hall name the magister who runs the watchtower, and the trail climbs toward the top floor. The lintel's carved words are copied for the scholars at Skyhold.
