## Setup

The Fallow Mire is a drowned valley of reeds, half-sunk farmhouses and causeways that vanish under black water. A grey fog hangs low over the reeds. A despair demon hovers above the path, and two shades drift in the mist between the stakes that once marked the crossing. The missing scouts were last seen somewhere in this grey.

## Twist

The despair demon's fog thickens, and the party loses its sense of direction. A voice that sounds like one of the scouts calls from the reeds. One ally is pulled toward the water, and the fight becomes a question of who can hold their footing and who can call them back.

## Preparation pays off

If search parties walked the causeways (project mire-search-parties), the party knows the safe stakes and has a rope line ready for the marsh. Trained companions keep their nerve in the fog, so the call from the reeds is easier to ignore. Unspent training sharpens strikes at the demon when it dips low enough to reach.

## Aftermath

The fog thins as the demon falls or flees. If the scouts are found alive, they are carried back to the causeway and given a warm fire and a long rest. If any of them is lost to the water, the search continues on another day, and Skyhold keeps the stakes in place for whoever goes next.
