## Setup

A sea cave sits at the foot of the cliffs, its mouth wide enough for a single rowing boat. Crates of stolen cargo are stacked on the shingle, and three Venatori gladiators stand guard with their backs to the surf. The climb down is steep and the stones are slick with spray. The ground inside the cave is flat and firm, which suits a heavy charge.

## Twist

The tide turns while the fight is still on. Water pours through the cove mouth, cutting off the retreat and forcing the party to fight in knee-deep water where every step shifts under them.

## Preparation pays off

If the storm coast patrols are posted (project storm-coast-patrols), a signal fire on the cliff warns the party before they go down, and a patrol drops a rope to speed the climb. Unspent training keeps the Inquisitor's balance steady on the wet rock, so the fight is decided by strength rather than luck. If the Chargers' cargo was already tracked, the party knows which crate to watch.

## Aftermath

The cove is cleared, and the Chargers take back what can be carried before the tide closes it again. The gladiators' papers name a buyer on the coast road, and Iron Bull notes the price on the cargo for later. Patrol posts keep watch on the cliff path through the night.
