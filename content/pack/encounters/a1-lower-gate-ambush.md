## Setup

Night, sleet, torchlight on wet stone. The paymaster's hired Venatori wait in
the gatehouse while a supply cart blocks the road. Narrow ground favours a
shield; the gatehouse stairs give an archer height.

## Twist

The spellbinder sets the cart alight to cover the paymaster's escape, splitting
the fight between stopping the fire and stopping the runner.

## Preparation pays off

If Cullen's drilled guards are posted (project drill-the-garrison), two
soldiers join the fight and the paymaster cannot slip past them. Unspent
training already sharpens the Inquisitor's attacks through the sheet; a high
supply store means extra healing potions in the guardhouse.

## Aftermath

The paymaster is caught or escapes with a burn on one hand; either way the
Tevinter seal is left behind. Garrison morale rises if no guard fell.
