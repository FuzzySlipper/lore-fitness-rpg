## Setup

The top chamber of the watchtower is circular and open to the sky, with a ring of old Tevinter lamps on the walls. Magister Aurelian Tharsis stands at a table of papers, flanked by two wraiths drawn up from a rift in the floor. Tharsis himself fights as the ruleset's Venatori Magister. Tharsis speaks as though he expects the Inquisition to arrive at this hour.

## Twist

Tharsis strikes a silver bell, and the rift in the floor widens. The wraiths step through it toward the party, and Tharsis begins a ritual that will collapse the chamber floor, leaving the Inquisitor to choose who holds the wraiths and who breaks his concentration.

## Preparation pays off

If the merchant princes were swayed (project sway-merchant-princes), Tharsis has lost two sources of coin, and no reinforcements answer his bell. If the network was swept (project sweep-the-network), the party knows the rift's anchor and can cut it while the wraiths are still in the hall. Unspent training keeps the party's footing on the uneven floor near the rift.

## Aftermath

If Tharsis falls, his papers are the last proof of the red lyrium plan, and the banners go up above the tower at dusk. If he escapes wounded, the Inquisition holds the watchtower and learns his route south. Either way, the Inquisition's march on Adamant can begin.
