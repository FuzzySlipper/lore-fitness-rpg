## Setup

A stone waystation on the courier road, with a stable yard, a barn and a gate of heavy planks. Raiders have broken the gate and hold the yard, where a courier's cart waits with its horses half-harnessed. Two gladiators hold the gate and two spellbinders stand on the barn roof, where they can see the whole yard.

## Twist

The spellbinders bind the barn doors with a ward, and the stable horses bolt through the yard in a panic. The horses block the gate and trample the Inquisitor's line, so the party must choose between clearing the yard and reaching the barn before the courier's ledger goes up in flames.

## Preparation pays off

If the supply ledger was audited (project audit-the-stores), the party knows exactly which crates belong to the waystation and which were forged. The gate crew can be sent round by the back lane, giving the party a second approach. Relief for the crossroads, if it was funded, means water and lanterns for the couriers who escaped the barn. Unspent training makes it easier to hold the line against the horses.

## Aftermath

The couriers are alive, and the ledger is saved if the party reached the barn in time. The raiders' papers list stops along the courier road, which becomes the next map for the Inquisition. A courier who was hurt is taken to the Inquisition's healers and asks, quietly, to be remembered for the trip.
