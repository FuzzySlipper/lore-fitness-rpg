## Setup

The iron gate of the quarry-fortress stands at the top of a switchback climb, its stone veined with glowing red lyrium. Two Red Templars man the gate, and a horror, a templar fused with lyrium, waits behind them in the gatehouse. Chains and a counterweight hold the portcullis, and the gatehouse arrow slits give the defenders a clean line of fire.

## Twist

Striking the gate's lyrium veins makes a spike of crystal erupt from the stone. The spike cuts the chains, and the portcullis begins to drop on the party. Someone has to hold the chain while the others fight the horror in the gatehouse.

## Preparation pays off

If the quarry was starved (project starve-the-quarry), the gate is short of men and the horror has fewer templars to hide behind. Ration crates are low, so the defenders hesitate. Fortified walls at Skyhold (project fortify-skyhold) mean the Inquisition's own gate is safe while its army goes up the road. Unspent training lets the Inquisitor hold the chain steady under the strain.

## Aftermath

If the gate falls, the quarry road opens, and the defenders who surrender speak of Ilse Varn and her orders. If the gate holds for the defenders, the Inquisition withdraws to plan a second approach. Either way, the gatehouse is left in the Inquisition's hands for the next push.
