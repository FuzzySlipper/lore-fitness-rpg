## Setup

A cluster of refugee tents sits around a dry well at the crossroads. Above the well, a seam in the air hums and flickers, leaking Fade mist into the dusk. Shades drift out of the seam first, and wraiths follow once the light begins to fail. Tall grass and a collapsed cart limit where anyone can stand.

## Twist

When a shade is struck, the seam flares and pulls loose tent cloth and debris into a whirl. The whirl blocks the path between the party and the refugee families, and the fight splits into two problems: holding the line and keeping the families out of the wind.

## Preparation pays off

If Relief for the Crossroads has been funded (project hinterlands-relief), barrels of clean water and a ring of lanterns already stand around the camp. The lanterns mark the safe ground, so the families can be moved behind them without a fight over every step. Unspent training keeps the party's footing firm in the grass, and their strikes land cleaner on the shades.

## Aftermath

The seam is marked with salt and stakes until a proper rift team can close it. The refugees who were moved to safety stay in the chapel for the night, and the crossroads keeps its lanterns lit. Anyone who fell in the grass is carried back to camp, where the healers are waiting.
