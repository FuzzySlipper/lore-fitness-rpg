## Setup

A small rift opens in a hinterlands field beside a broken fence, its edges rippling like heat over stone. Two shades drift out of it to wander the grass, and a wraith hangs close to the seam. The ground is open and quiet, and the nearest farm lies a short walk away.

## Twist

The rift pulls at the nearest person's cloak, and a shade lunges for the same spot. The party must choose whether to hold their ground or pull back and let the rift settle, while the shades keep moving.

## Preparation pays off

Unspent training keeps the party steady at the rift's edge. Supplies stored at Skyhold mean a lantern and salt are waiting in the wagon, and the lantern line keeps the grass clear. This operation may be repeated, so a party that prepared well last time will find the field calmer.

## Aftermath

The rift is closed or left marked with stakes for a later visit. The farmers thank the party with bread and a warm fire, and the field returns to its usual quiet. Nothing here decides the story; the Inquisition simply has one less seam to watch.
