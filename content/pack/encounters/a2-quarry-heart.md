## Setup

The quarry pit is a huge cut in the mountain, its walls striped with bands of red lyrium. At the bottom, a red templar behemoth stands on the broken floor, its plating grown over with crystal. Gantries cross the pit high above, and a broad ramp leads down to the flat ground where the fight will be decided.

## Twist

Ilse Varn appears on the gantry and calls down an order, and the behemoth pauses between strikes. Her words give the Inquisitor a moment to choose: press the attack, or offer her terms and hope the behemoth stands down.

## Preparation pays off

If the quarry was starved (project starve-the-quarry), the behemoth's crew is thin, and the lyrium it draws on is running short. Its strikes are slower, and the party has room to work around its feet. Unspent training helps the Inquisitor keep clear of the crystal spines, and a fortified ramp at Skyhold lets reinforcements come down behind the line.

## Aftermath

Whether the behemoth falls or is halted, the pit is secured. Varn's fate is decided by what the Inquisitor says and does on the gantry: she may be taken prisoner, left to the lyrium, or allowed to walk away. The red lyrium is sealed in crates, and the road back to Skyhold is marked for the march.
