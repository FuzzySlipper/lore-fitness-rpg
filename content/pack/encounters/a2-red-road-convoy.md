## Setup

Open plains under a hard sky, with a convoy of three lyrium carts trundling along the old road. Red Templars ride escort, their armour glinting with red lyrium inlay. There is little cover, and the road runs along a shallow slope that gives the riders the high ground. A sudden shift in the wind carries the stink of hot metal across the field.

## Twist

One of the templars shouts a signal, and the drivers cut the traces. A loaded cart rolls down the slope and spills crates across the road, leaving the party a choice between the templars and the rolling wagon.

## Preparation pays off

If the lyrium wagons were traced (project trace-lyrium-wagons), the party knows which cart carries the real cargo and can go for it first. The false floors have been marked, so the cargo can be taken without a long search. Unspent supplies mean the Inquisition's carts can follow the convoy at a distance, ready to bring the captured crates home.

## Aftermath

The templars that surrender give up manifests and a name for the quarry's quartermaster. The red lyrium is carried back under guard, and the carts are weighed at Skyhold so Hadley Brisk can enter the true numbers. Any templar who breaks away is left to ride toward the quarry, where the road will lead the Inquisition next.
