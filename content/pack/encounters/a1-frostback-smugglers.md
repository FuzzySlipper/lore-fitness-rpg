## Setup

Dusk in the high pass, wind driving fine snow across a switchback road. A pack mule stands tied beside a shuttered shelter, its panniers heavy with sealed red lyrium crates. The smugglers have chosen a narrow cut between two boulders where riders can only pass single file. Ice coats the rock, and anyone in heavy armour slides on every step.

## Twist

A crate splits when the mule panics, and a cloud of lyrium dust drifts across the cut. Spells cast inside it sputter, throw sparks or land wide. The spellbinder seems pleased by the mess and keeps casting regardless.

## Preparation pays off

If the passes were scouted (project scout-frostback-passes), the Inquisition knows the cut and sends riders around the far side, so one smuggler is already pinned when the fight begins. Unspent training helps the party keep their feet on the ice. Supplies set aside at Skyhold mean warm packs and bandages waiting in the shelter.

## Aftermath

If the smugglers are beaten, the mule and crates go back to Skyhold under guard, and Hadley Brisk gets a fresh entry for her ledger. If one smuggler escapes, a trail of dropped lyrium shards leads higher into the pass. Either way, the Venatori seal found in the snow is evidence for the courier network.
