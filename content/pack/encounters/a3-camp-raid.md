## Setup

Night in the Western Approach, with the staging camps spread across a long valley. Tents, supply carts and horse lines run in rows toward a low ridge. A rage demon has come up out of the ruins behind the camp, and three shades slip between the carts. Torches light the tent lines, and every spark seems to drift toward the dry grass.

## Twist

The rage demon sets a tent alight with a single blow, and the fire spreads along the rows of canvas. The party must fight the demon and the shades while fire crews race to stop the flames from reaching the supply carts.

## Preparation pays off

If the camps were laid out properly (project western-approach-camps), fire breaks and guard rotas are already in place, so the blaze stops at the first break. Supplies set aside at Skyhold mean water barrels and spare shields are ready at the centre of the camp. Unspent training lets the Inquisitor's party move quickly between the carts without losing their footing in the smoke.

## Aftermath

The camps are saved, and the carts are counted for the next march. A few scorched tents and a broken horse line are all that remain of the raid. The ash carries the mark of a Venatori ritual, and the scouts report that the ruins behind the camp were recently disturbed.
