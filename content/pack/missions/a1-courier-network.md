## Premise

The route map from the Frostback pass ends at a waystation on a lonely Hinterlands road: an inn, a stable and a locked cellar. Every courier in the smugglers' network passes through it, and its manager is paid from a distant account. Cassandra, Bull and Sera are ready to break the network in one night.

## Stakes

If the waystation falls, the network is cut and the paymaster exposed. If it is only scattered, the couriers will rebuild with better hiding. A paymaster who learns the Inquisition is close will run.

## Beats

1. The approach: the inn looks ordinary, with merchants, a bard and a quiet stable. Sera is sure that three of the merchants are watching.
2. Bull and Cassandra disagree about how to take the building. Sera has already been inside and has a third plan.
3. The inn's guests are not what they seem. Venatori guards close the doors.
4. In the cellar: couriers' seals, sealed letters, and a ledger of payments signed A.T., with a full name written on a covering note: Magister Aurelian Tharsis.
5. A rider escapes into the dark with word of the raid. Tharsis will know before dawn.

## Choices

- Storm the inn, wait out the night, or turn a courier: storming ends the network now; waiting catches the rider; turning a courier feeds false news upward.
- Burn the ledger, keep it, or copy it: keeping gives the war table proof; copying lets Sera's friends read it quietly.
- Let the inn's staff go or hold them: letting them go protects the innocent and spreads rumour; holding them gives Leliana a lead.

## Encounters

a1-waystation-raid.

## Outcomes

The courier network is broken and Magister Aurelian Tharsis is named as paymaster. Skyhold's stores are safer, the war table knows its enemy, and Cassandra, Bull and Sera have fought together. Unlocks act two: a2-red-road and the Tevinter-focused missions.

## Low-energy version

Play the planning scene, not the raid. At a table in Skyhold the Inquisitor, Cassandra, Bull and Sera argue the plan with a map and a candle, then hear Cassandra's report the next morning, with the ledger and the name on the table.
