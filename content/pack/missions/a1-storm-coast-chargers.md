## Premise

Iron Bull's Chargers have a contract in the rain: a smugglers' cove on the Storm Coast that has been moving crates for Venatori buyers. Bull asks the Inquisitor to come along, partly for the fight and partly because the Chargers want to see who they are really working for.

## Stakes

The Chargers are a loose, loyal crew who could drift off on a better offer. The cove is a dock for Tevinter ships, and if it stays open the Frostback route has a sea door.

## Beats

1. Rain, cold surf and the Chargers' camp. Bull introduces the crew in a cheerful string of insults that mean affection.
2. The crew has a plan, loud and violent. A quieter option exists, using the cliff path and the tide.
3. At the cove, a cargo crate is open: red-wrapped shards, and a manifest in Tevinter script.
4. The smugglers are Venatori professionals, not hired thugs.
5. By the fire afterwards, Bull asks the Inquisitor an offhand question about what they would do with a crew like his.

## Choices

- Loud assault or cliff path and tide: the assault is fast and costly for the Chargers; the path is slower, and the crew learns the Inquisitor plans around lives.
- Burn the crates or load them for study: burning is clean; study gives Leliana something to read, at a risk Bull cheerfully describes as "interesting".
- Keep the manifest private or share it with the Chargers: sharing shows trust and lets Bull's crew tie the cove to Tevinter ships.

## Encounters

a1-storm-coast-cove.

## Outcomes

The cove is shut and the Chargers stand a little closer to the Inquisition. The manifest reaches the war table and unlocks the war-table project storm-coast-patrols.

## Low-energy version

Play it in the camp, in rain under a tarp. Bull and the Inquisitor go through the Chargers' plan over a map and a bottle, argue the options, and choose. The cove is seen as a skirmish told afterward by whoever lost the bet on how it would go.
