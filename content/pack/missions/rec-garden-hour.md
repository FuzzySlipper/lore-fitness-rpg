## Premise

The garden at Skyhold is overgrown and quiet, and a bench in the corner catches the afternoon sun. Cassandra is there with a book she is pretending not to enjoy. Solas arrives later with a sketchbook and a comment about the plants.

## Stakes

None. It is an hour in a quiet place with two people who rarely get to sit still.

## Beats

1. Cassandra offers a page of her book, an adventure romance she defends gruffly.
2. Solas names a plant and the story behind it, and goes quiet halfway.
3. The two of them disagree about whether the old ivy should be cut, politely and for a good while.
4. A bird lands on the wall and all three stop to watch it.
5. The light changes and nobody mentions leaving.

## Choices

- Read aloud, listen or sketch: all are welcome.
- Side with Cassandra on the ivy, with Solas, or with the ivy: the ivy is not a political question, though it's treated as one.
- Stay for the sunset or leave early: the garden will be here tomorrow.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

Cassandra and Solas each remember the Inquisitor sat with them. The garden is a little tidier or a little wilder. Nothing at the war table changes.

## Low-energy version

Play it as a single page of the book, one plant, one comment and a long silence, with the Inquisitor on the bench and the garden doing the rest.
