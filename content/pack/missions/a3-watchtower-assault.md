## Premise

Magister Tharsis has gone to ground in a ruined Tevinter watchtower in the Western Approach, ringed by Venatori and red-lyrium-touched guards. The Inquisition's army is behind the Inquisitor, and the finale begins here. Any companions may be swapped in for Cassandra, Bull and Dorian.

## Stakes

If Tharsis lives, the march on Adamant will be hounded by demons and doubt. If he falls, the Venatori's southern plan falls with him and the Inquisition marches with its supply lines intact.

## Beats

1. The approach: a bleak plain, the tower against the sky, and the army deployed in quiet order.
2. The gate: a gladiator-guarded arch where a transformed templar holds the way.
3. Inside, the tower's old Tevinter halls have been refitted as a magister's study, calm to the point of insult.
4. Tharsis receives the Inquisitor as though they were a late guest, and offers terms.
5. The offer fails, and the watchtower's upper chamber becomes a Fade-shot arena with Tharsis at its centre.

## Choices

- Assault or take the tower by negotiated stealth: assault is loud and quick; stealth is slower and keeps the army back.
- Spare Tharsis if he yields or end him: sparing yields intelligence and a bitter prisoner; ending him ends the threat.
- Honour Varn's fate: if she was saved, her testimony is heard; if killed, Cullen's grief shows; if left, rumours turn to dread.

## Encounters

a3-watchtower-gate, a3-tharsis.

## Outcomes

Tharsis falls or is taken, and the Venatori's southern plan collapses. The Western Approach is safe for the march, and the war table can plan Adamant. Unlocks a3-banners-raised.

## Low-energy version

Play the eve of the assault: the Inquisitor and companions at the edge of the camp, talking through the plan and each person's part. The fight is a dawn dispatch.
