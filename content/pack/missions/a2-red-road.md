## Premise

Leliana's scouts have traced the courier network's last shipments to a dusty road across the Exalted Plains, where wagons under tarpaulin move at night. The cargo hums faintly and stains the wheel ruts crimson. Someone upstream is mining red lyrium in bulk.

## Stakes

If the convoys keep flowing, every Red Templar in the south is fed and every Venatori payroll is covered. The wagons are also the first real thread back to whoever holds the mine.

## Beats

1. Cassandra and Dorian argue over the map: she wants the convoy stopped at once, he wants to know where it is going before anyone draws a sword.
2. A ruined way-shrine beside the road offers a vantage point, and a cold camp where earlier travellers were clearly driven off.
3. The convoy arrives with a Red Templar escort whose armour is more crystal than steel.
4. Among the wreckage afterward: a waybill stamped with a quarry mark, and a captain's seal Cullen will recognise.
5. Dorian, uneasy near the cargo, says what the crystals do to the air around them.

## Choices

- Ambush at the shrine or take the wagons mid-road: the ambush is cleaner and keeps drivers alive to question; the open fight is faster but scatters evidence.
- Burn the cargo or carry some home: burning denies the enemy; carrying it lets Dorian and the scholars study it, with a risk Cassandra will name.
- Question the drivers gently or hard: gentle gets the quarry's location and a rumour of its captain; hard gets it quicker and leaves the survivors bitter.

## Outcomes

The quarry at Redcrag and its holder, Knight-Captain Ilse Varn, are named. Leliana's agents mark the road, and war-table project trace-lyrium-wagons becomes available. Unlocks a2-old-comrade, a2-graves-supply, a2-solas-rift and a2-quarry-siege.

## Low-energy version

Play the planning scene: the Inquisitor stands over the map with Cassandra and Dorian choosing where to meet the convoy, while Leliana's report supplies what happened on the road. Keep the fight to a single reported paragraph and spend the scene on the waybill and the seal.
