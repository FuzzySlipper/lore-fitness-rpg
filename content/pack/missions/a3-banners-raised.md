## Premise

The watchtower has fallen, and Skyhold fills with music, banners and the smell of roasting. Every companion has a corner of the hall and a story. The Inquisition is ready to march on Adamant, and tonight is for gathering before the road.

## Stakes

Nothing breaks if the night is quiet, but a bad evening would leave the army tired and the companions unspoken-to. The march begins when Skyhold is rested.

## Beats

1. The great hall at dusk, with new banners being raised and every soldier trying not to look proud.
2. Companions drift through: Bull with a cask, Sera with a prank, Dorian with an opinion on the wine.
3. Cullen and Cassandra offer quiet words, Leliana a map, and Solas a long look at the sky.
4. Josephine announces the feast; Blackwall asks the Inquisitor to say something to the troops.
5. The last banner goes up, bearing the Inquisition's sigil, and the march is named aloud: Adamant.

## Choices

- Speak to the whole hall or visit each companion: speaking inspires the troops; visiting deepens each friendship.
- Linger at the feast or slip away to the battlements: lingering builds the hold's unity; slipping away gives a private word with someone.
- Name the march's first day or leave it for the war table: naming it commits the army; leaving it keeps options.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

Season one ends with the Inquisition rested, allied and ready. The march on Adamant is planned for season two, and every companion's mood is recorded in the war table. Skyhold remembers the night.

## Low-energy version

This mission is the low-energy version: a quiet evening of conversation, with no fights, travel or deadlines. It can be played in a few minutes or stretched into a long night.
