## Premise

Leliana tells the Inquisitor, in the rookery after hours, that one of her agents has been feeding information to Tharsis. She has not yet confronted the agent, and she wants the Inquisitor in the room.

## Stakes

The leak explains why the staging camps were hit and why some of Leliana's earlier reports went stale. If it goes unaddressed, the network's trust fails; if handled harshly, Leliana's people learn what loyalty costs.

## Beats

1. Leliana lays out the evidence calmly, and only her stillness shows how much it matters.
2. The agent, a capable young courier with a family far away, is invited to supper under a pretext.
3. The conversation circles what the agent has been told and what has been offered.
4. The agent's reasons are neither simple nor stupid, and Leliana listens.
5. Whatever follows, a message must be sent to Tharsis, or not.

## Choices

- Turn the agent back as a double: feeding Tharsis false reports helps the assault and puts the agent at risk.
- Send the agent away: removes the leak and loses a skilled courier.
- Offer protection for the agent's family: costs resources and earns a loyal agent, and the network watches how it is done.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

The leak is closed one way or another. Leliana's network is sharper and more wary, and war-table project sweep-the-network becomes meaningful. What Tharsis believes about the Inquisition's plans shapes the watchtower assault.

## Low-energy version

Play the supper only: three people at a table, a pot of tea, and a difficult conversation. No pursuit, no disguise, no travel.
