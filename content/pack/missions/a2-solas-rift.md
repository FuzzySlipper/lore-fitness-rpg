## Premise

On the Exalted Plains a rift has opened above a battlefield, and Solas wants to study it before it is sealed. He says the rift is unusually quiet, which he does not find comforting.

## Stakes

If the rift is closed too hastily, whatever it is echoing is lost; if it is left open, the plains fill with shades. Solas's easy competence is also on trial, because he knows more than he lets on.

## Beats

1. Solas leads the Inquisitor across the field, naming the old fighting in a way that sounds more like remembrance than history.
2. The rift's edge shows images of a battle that does not match any account in Skyhold's library.
3. He asks the Inquisitor to hold the line while he reads the tear, and shades gather as he works.
4. The rift's quiet breaks, and what was an echo becomes a threat.
5. Afterward he says something simple about the Fade and the dead, then changes the subject.

## Choices

- Close the rift at once or let Solas finish reading it: closing is safe; waiting gives him time and brings more shades.
- Ask Solas what he saw or leave it: asking gets a careful, partial answer; leaving it earns a quiet thank-you.
- Mark the field for the scholars or leave it undisturbed: marking helps the Inquisition understand the plains; leaving it respects the dead.

## Encounters

op-rift.

## Outcomes

The rift is sealed, and the Inquisitor owns a small, odd piece of Solas's trust. The war table notes a stretch of Exalted Plains safe for patrols. Nothing is said that should not be.

## Low-energy version

Play it as the pause after the rift is closed: the Inquisitor sits with Solas on the plain while he explains what the quiet was, and the fight is told in one or two lines.
