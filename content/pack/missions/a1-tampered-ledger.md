## Premise

Quartermaster Hadley Brisk brings the Inquisitor a supply ledger whose numbers
are neat, plausible and wrong. Grain and iron leave Skyhold on paper that never
arrives anywhere. Someone inside the fortress is feeding a smuggling route.

## Stakes

If the leak continues, the winter stores fail and the Inquisition starts the
season hungry. Worse, whoever forged the ledger knows Skyhold's routines.

## Beats

1. Brisk walks the Inquisitor through the discrepancies in the cold storeroom, nervous and defensive.
2. Cullen wants a barracks inspection; Leliana wants to watch and wait.
3. A clue: a supply wagon logged as returning from Haven, which no longer exists.
4. A night-time confrontation at the lower gate with a frightened clerk and the person paying him.
5. The paymaster's seal bears a Tevinter mark and the initials A.T.

## Choices

- Inspect openly (Cullen) or watch quietly (Leliana): open inspection catches the clerk fast but warns the paymaster; watching risks another shipment but catches both.
- Spare or punish the clerk: mercy gains an informant for act one; punishment reassures the garrison.
- Tell the whole war table or keep it close: telling Josephine lets her quietly warn suppliers.

## Encounters

a1-lower-gate-ambush (only if the paymaster's guards are cornered).

## Outcomes

Skyhold's stores are secured. The name Aurelian Tharsis enters Leliana's
reports. Unlocks a1-frostback-pass and war-table project audit-the-stores.

## Low-energy version

Play the investigation as a conversation over the ledger in Josephine's office:
the Inquisitor compares entries, Brisk explains, and the culprit is exposed by
reasoning. The confrontation happens off-screen, reported by Leliana.
