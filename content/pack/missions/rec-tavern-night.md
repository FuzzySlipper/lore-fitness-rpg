## Premise

The Herald's Rest is full, loud and warm. Bull has saved a corner table, Sera has a game she insists is simple, and Dorian arrived with a bottle he says is better than the ale and is probably right. Nobody has anywhere else to be.

## Stakes

None. A good night is its own reward.

## Beats

1. The corner table: Bull deals a card game that Sera has changed the rules of, twice already.
2. Dorian offers an elaborate toast. It is really about the people at the table.
3. A bard takes the floor and Sera takes offence on the song's behalf, then sings along.
4. A quiet hour: stories of home, or old jobs, or the funniest thing that ever happened to someone at this table.
5. The tavern thins out. Someone is asleep on the bench, and nobody minds.

## Choices

- Play the card game, tell a story or just listen: all are good.
- Pick sides in Sera and Dorian's argument or sit between them: both are fun.
- Stay until the end or go early: the night keeps going without you.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

Three companions are a little closer and the tavern remembers the Inquisitor kindly. Nothing at the war table changes, and that is the point.

## Low-energy version

Play it as a short evening, one drink, one story and one round of cards, with the Inquisitor in the best chair. It works as a ten-minute scene.
