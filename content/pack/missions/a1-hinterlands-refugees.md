## Premise

A crossroads village has swollen with farmers pushed off their land by the war. Josephine has letters from three hamlets begging for grain, and the Inquisition has a wagon to spare. A small rift has opened on the road, and it will not stay small if nobody tends to it.

## Stakes

If the villagers are left to fend for themselves, they drift toward bandits or Venatori coin. A fed crossroads becomes a friendly one, with eyes on the roads.

## Beats

1. The wagon creaks into the crossroads at dusk. Blackwall counts the faces and the empty hands; Cassandra counts the weapons.
2. A village elder and a stubborn miller disagree about how the grain should be shared out. Someone has to settle it.
3. A child reports glowing air by the old mill road. The rift is feeble but growing.
4. Fade creatures drift out of the green light. The wagon must be kept safe behind the line.
5. Afterwards, the village offers what it has: a bed, a story, and a warning about strangers buying grain at too good a price.

## Choices

- Share grain by household or by need: households is fast and feels fair; need is slower but may keep the most vulnerable fed through winter.
- Take volunteers for the Inquisition or leave the village whole: recruits strengthen Cullen's numbers, but the village loses hands before the harvest.
- Press the miller about the strangers or let it lie: pressing finds a Venatori buyer; leaving it keeps the village trust.

## Encounters

a1-hinterlands-rift.

## Outcomes

The crossroads is a friendly place and its elder sends word to Skyhold. The note about the grain buyer reaches Leliana and later helps a1-courier-network. War-table project hinterlands-relief becomes available.

## Low-energy version

Play it as an evening in the village hall. The Inquisitor, Blackwall and Cassandra sit with the elder and the miller, hear the grievances, divide the grain and talk. The rift is closed off-screen by the garrison, reported over supper.
