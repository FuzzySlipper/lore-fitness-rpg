## Premise

Cut off from his red lyrium, Magister Aurelian Tharsis answers with a bolder stroke: demons loosed on the Inquisition's staging camps in the Western Approach. Tents burn under a sky that has gone the wrong colour, and Blackwall and Dorian are the nearest officers.

## Stakes

The camps hold the supplies and soldiers for the march on Adamant. If they fall, the march is delayed a season; if the soldiers panic, the camps are lost without a blow struck.

## Beats

1. The Inquisitor arrives to a camp already half-ablaze, with Blackwall organising a bucket line and Dorian wrestling with the tear that spawned the fire.
2. A rage demon wades through the quartermaster's tents, and every soldier it touches grows louder and angrier.
3. Dorian recognises the work: a Tevinter binding, not a wild rift, and someone must be holding the other end.
4. The binder is found, a hired mage with a ledger, and Tharsis's name is on the payment.
5. As dawn comes, the camps have to be rebuilt or abandoned, and the soldiers look to the Inquisitor.

## Choices

- Save the camp's stores or its people first: people keep morale and lose supplies; stores keep the march on schedule and cost lives.
- Question the binder or let Dorian break the binding: questioning gives Tharsis's location; breaking the binding saves the camp's rest of the night.
- Rebuild in place or move the camps: rebuilding is quick and a target; moving costs time and is safer.

## Encounters

a3-camp-raid.

## Outcomes

Tharsis's watchtower in the Western Approach is located. Camps are rebuilt or relocated, noted in war-table western-approach-camps. Unlocks a3-watchtower-assault. The binder's ledger gives Leliana a thread for a3-turned-agent.

## Low-energy version

Play the morning after: smoking tents, Blackwall and Dorian reporting, and the Inquisitor choosing how to rebuild. The raid is told as a short account, and the binder's ledger becomes a reading scene.
