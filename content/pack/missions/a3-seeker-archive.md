## Premise

Cassandra learns that the Venatori have been seeking a cache of old Seeker records held in a ruined outpost in the Western Approach. She is unsettled that her order's papers are of interest to Tharsis, and asks the Inquisitor to come with her.

## Stakes

The records hold old names, routes and safeguards. In Venatori hands they could unmask agents, expose caches and corrode the Chantry's few remaining allies.

## Beats

1. A long ride across scrub and wind-carved stone, with Cassandra silent and then suddenly talkative about her order.
2. The outpost is partly dug out already, with fresh tracks and tools left behind.
3. Inside, shelves of damp ledgers and one sealed chest that Cassandra will not open without someone watching.
4. A Venatori scout is found, caught between fleeing and finishing his task.
5. Cassandra decides what is kept, what is burned, and what is carried back.

## Choices

- Carry the records to Skyhold or burn them: carrying preserves history and risks capture; burning protects names and loses knowledge.
- Spare the scout or hold him: sparing lets him report what he saw; holding yields what Tharsis knows.
- Let Cassandra read first or read together: reading alone respects her order; reading together deepens trust.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

The Venatori lose a thread to Seeker secrets. Cassandra is steadier and trusts the Inquisitor with a story about her order. The war table gains a small collection of records or a long fire in the Western Approach.

## Low-energy version

Play the evening after the outpost: Cassandra at a campfire with the first ledger, talking over what to keep. The ride and the scout are brief reports.
