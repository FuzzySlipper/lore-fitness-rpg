## Premise

A scouting party sent to chart the lyrium road's southern spur has not reported in. The Fallow Mire is a grey, sodden place where the dead walk and the Fade is thin, and Bull and Solas disagree about whether the scouts fled, fell, or were taken.

## Stakes

If the scouts are alive, they know where the Red Templars rest between convoys. If they are not, Leliana loses her best picture of the southern road, and the Mire's despair spreads toward the settlements.

## Beats

1. The edge of the Mire: mist, bad ground, and Bull casually marking dry paths with a rope.
2. A scout's abandoned camp with the fire still smouldering and the notebooks wet but readable.
3. Solas senses something wrong with the air near a half-sunk chapel; the Mire's cold has a pattern.
4. A despair demon feeds on the scouts' fear in the old chapel, and one scout is still unbroken.
5. The survivors are brought out as the marsh dims, carrying the notes that point to Redcrag.

## Choices

- Follow the scouts' trail or Solas's reading of the cold: the trail is slower and surer; Solas's path is faster and takes the party near the demon.
- Burn the chapel or leave it: burning stops the haunting and destroys anything the scouts did not copy; leaving it preserves the notes and the danger.
- Escort the survivors home or leave a camp: escorting saves them for certain; a camp keeps the Mire watched.

## Encounters

a2-mire-despair.

## Outcomes

The scouts' route notes strengthen the lyrium road's map and Leliana's network. The war-table project mire-search-parties is available, and Bull's people gain a story to tell. Surviving scouts know the Inquisitor came for them.

## Low-energy version

Run the rescue as a briefing: Bull and Solas debate the scouts' fate at the war table, the Inquisitor picks the approach, and the report returns with the survivors and a short account of the chapel.
