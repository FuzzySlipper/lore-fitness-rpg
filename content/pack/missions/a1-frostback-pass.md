## Premise

The stolen Skyhold grain did not vanish; it went north. Leliana's scouts place a smugglers' trail in a high Frostback pass, where wagons slip between a shuttered toll-house and a cave mouth. Cassandra wants the ring cut off at its root, and Solas has noticed that the cave hums.

## Stakes

If the route stays open, red lyrium and stolen stores flow to the Venatori all winter and the Inquisition pays for it twice. A hasty assault may also destroy the evidence that points up the chain.

## Beats

1. The climb: thin air, old snow, and a pass the Inquisition does not yet control. Cassandra sets a hard pace; Solas points out where the trail is too tidy.
2. The toll-house: abandoned in appearance, but its ledgers are fresh. Crates of Skyhold grain share a cellar with wrapped, faintly glowing shards.
3. Solas senses the shards sing wrongly and warns against handling them without cloth and care.
4. The smugglers return with a wagon and find the Inquisition waiting, or find the Inquisitor already inside.
5. After the fight, a courier satchel holds a route map ending at a waystation in the Hinterlands.

## Choices

- Ambush at the toll-house or intercept on the trail: the ambush protects the evidence but risks a loud trap; the trail is cleaner but lets the wagon's contents scatter.
- Destroy the shards or carry them out: Solas favours burial in the cave, Cassandra wants proof for the war table. Destruction is safe; proof sharpens Leliana's reports.
- Spare a wounded driver for questions or send him down the mountain: a living witness names the waystation's cook; a quick release keeps the camp quiet.

## Encounters

a1-frostback-smugglers.

## Outcomes

The pass is contested rather than closed. The route map names the waystation, unlocking a1-courier-network, and Leliana's scouts report the tracks of a second, quieter smuggler. War-table project scout-frostback-passes becomes worth ordering.

## Low-energy version

Play it as a war-table scene at Skyhold. Cassandra and Solas return from the pass and report in turn; the Inquisitor questions them, examines the satchel and one wrapped shard, and decides what happens to the shards and the driver. The fight is summarised in two sentences.
