## Premise

An outpost in the foothills needs blankets, nails and salt. Blackwall has volunteered to lead the mule train and invites the Inquisitor to come along, since the road is long and the company is better than the weather.

## Stakes

An outpost without supplies empties out by spring. An outpost remembered by the Inquisition holds.

## Beats

1. Loading the mules at dawn. Blackwall knows each mule by name and temperament.
2. A washed-out stretch of track requires a decision: unload, repack, or take the long way round.
3. A pair of travellers ask to walk with the train for safety. They are probably honest.
4. The outpost greets the train with relief and a pot of something hot.
5. On the ride back Blackwall talks about the road, or doesn't.

## Choices

- Repack on the spot or take the long way: repacking is quick and tiring; the long way is slow and safe.
- Let the travellers join or send them on: joining may bring news, or trouble.
- Leave extra at the outpost or save it for the next stop: generosity here leaves less to spend elsewhere.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

The outpost keeps its watch and sends back a letter of thanks. Blackwall has a good opinion of the Inquisitor's grip on the practical. A repeatable operation; the war table notes the outpost stocked.

## Low-energy version

Play it as a conversation in the stable yard at Skyhold while the mules are loaded: Blackwall tells the story of the road and the outpost and the Inquisitor decides what goes on the mules.
