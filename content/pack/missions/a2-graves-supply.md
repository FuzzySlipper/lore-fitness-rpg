## Premise

Skyhold's siege engines and the staging camps will need timber, and the Emerald Graves has plenty of it. The people who live in the forest are cautious about outsiders cutting it. Sera knows how to talk to people who dislike nobles, and Solas knows which stands are old enough to leave alone.

## Stakes

The Inquisition needs wood, and it needs the forest's goodwill more than it needs any one stand of trees. A clumsy deal would leave a quiet grudge that follows the supply line for the rest of the war.

## Beats

1. A woodland trail where Sera spots the watchers before anyone else does, and teases about it.
2. A parley at a lumber camp: the foresters list what the Inquisition may take and what it may not.
3. Solas walks the Inquisitor through ruins grown into the trees, noting what the old stand remembers.
4. Bandits, or a rival buyer, are paying more and cutting deeper, and the foresters have noticed.
5. A deal is struck or broken over a shared meal that nobody wants to be the first to end.

## Choices

- Pay in coin or in labour: coin is quick; labour builds the foresters' stake in the Inquisition and costs time.
- Take the cheaper deep-forest wood or the dearer managed stand: deep wood saves supplies and angers the old stand; managed wood keeps the peace.
- Expose the rival buyer or buy him out: exposing wins the foresters' loyalty; buying him out secures stock.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

Skyhold's timber stocks rise, or the forest keeps its distance, depending on the deal. The foresters send word to other woodland camps. Sera and Solas each remember how the Inquisitor handled the exchange.

## Low-energy version

Play it as a single negotiation: the Inquisitor, Sera and Solas at a long table in a lumber camp, with a stack of terms and a pot of something hot. No walking, no brawl.
