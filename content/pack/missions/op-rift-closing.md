## Premise

A small tear in the Veil has opened in a field the farmers have started to avoid. It is not dangerous yet; with the Inquisitor's mark it can be sealed before it grows. Solas offers to come along and talk the Inquisitor through what he can see.

## Stakes

A rift left alone slowly poisons a field and draws things to it. The mark makes the Inquisitor useful and a little lonely.

## Beats

1. Solas walks the edge of the field, noting where the grass has stopped growing and the air tastes of metal.
2. Fade creatures drift out, drawn to the Inquisitor's mark. The line must hold.
3. Solas explains the structure of the tear, not as a lecture but as someone thinking aloud.
4. The mark answers, the green light closes, and the field goes quiet.
5. A farmer comes by with a thermos of something warm and no questions.

## Choices

- Close it at once or study it first: closing is safe; studying with Solas teaches more but lets the rift grow.
- Ask Solas what he sees or let the silence stand: both are welcome.
- Take the farmer's offering or decline: the offer is kindness either way.

## Encounters

op-rift.

## Outcomes

The field heals slowly and the farmers return. Solas has a little more to say about the Veil next time. A repeatable operation; the war table notes a rift closed.

## Low-energy version

Play it as a walk with Solas to the edge of the field, a talk about what the Fade looks like at close range, and a single calm closing. The Fade creatures stay at the horizon.
