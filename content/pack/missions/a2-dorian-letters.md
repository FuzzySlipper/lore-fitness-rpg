## Premise

Dorian receives three letters in a week from Minrathous, none in a hand he likes. One is from an old friend, one is official, and one has no sender and a seal that is faintly wrong. He asks the Inquisitor to help him decide what to answer.

## Stakes

The letters may carry real information about Magister Tharsis and the Venatori's finances, or they may be bait meant to unsettle the Inquisition's most visible Tevinter. Dorian's trust in his own judgment is also at stake.

## Beats

1. Dorian, in the library or the garden, lays the three letters out and pretends to be casual about it.
2. The friend's letter is warm, guarded and obviously written by someone who is being read over.
3. The official letter is a polite offer from his family to talk, and the courtesy is its own pressure.
4. The unsigned letter names Tharsis's accountants. Reading it against the ledger from the Skyhold stores is a puzzle in itself.
5. Dorian drafts replies aloud and asks what the Inquisitor would say to each.

## Choices

- Answer the friend honestly or carefully: honesty may cost the friend safety; care keeps the channel open but thin.
- Meet the family's offer or let it lapse: meeting it opens a quiet line to Josephine; letting it lapse keeps Dorian's distance and his anger.
- Hand the unsigned letter to Leliana or test it first with Dorian: Leliana's people verify it faster; Dorian's own check proves it a real lead but is slower.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

Dorian is steadier and more at home in Skyhold. The war table gains the project tevinter-correspondence, and Leliana learns the shape of Tharsis's money. Nothing about Dorian's family is settled, and he says so.

## Low-energy version

This mission is made for a tired day. Play it entirely as conversation, with Dorian reading lines aloud and the Inquisitor weighing replies. No travel, no combat, and it ends whenever the three letters are answered or set aside.
