## Premise

Sera finds a folded note under her door and refuses to say who sent it. The scrawl hints that someone in Skyhold's kitchens and stable yards has seen the smugglers' clerk talking to strangers. Sera's loose network of friends is already asking questions, and she wants the Inquisitor to follow where it leads.

## Stakes

The people who notice the most in Skyhold are those nobody looks at. If the Inquisition brushes them off, it loses its best ears. If it treats the lead with care, they keep talking.

## Beats

1. Sera drags the Inquisitor into the kitchen at an odd hour, whispering and laughing, to meet a stable hand who "may not know anything".
2. The stable hand knows a carter who bought drinks for the clerk the week before the ledger went wrong.
3. Sera has a plan for questioning the carter that involves a prank, a cheese wheel and a distraction.
4. The carter talks, frightened of the wrong people, and gives a name for a courier who rides north.
5. Sera asks, too casually, what the Inquisitor thinks of people like her friends.

## Choices

- Follow Sera's prank or ask plainly: the prank earns trust and a good story; plain questions may be slower but keep the carter calm.
- Protect the carter or bring him to Leliana: protection keeps the network open; Leliana learns faster but the kitchens go quiet.
- Tell Josephine, Leliana or no one: only Sera's friends know now, and each telling changes who answers next time.

## Encounters

none.

## Outcomes

The courier's name goes to the war table, and Sera's friends hold the Inquisition in good opinion. A courier route into the Hinterlands is later easier to trace.

## Low-energy version

Play it as a quiet evening in the kitchen. Sera, the stable hand and the Inquisitor share a stew and a lot of jokes while the real information comes out sideways. No prank needed, unless it's a small one.
