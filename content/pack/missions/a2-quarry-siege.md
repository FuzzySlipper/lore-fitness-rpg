## Premise

Redcrag Quarry is a cut in a grey hillside where red lyrium grows through stone and men. Knight-Captain Ilse Varn holds it with the last of her faithful templars, and the Inquisition's army is at the gates. Cullen, Cassandra and Bull lead the assault.

## Stakes

If Redcrag stands, the lyrium road keeps feeding the enemy. If it falls badly, the Inquisition kills a woman who might have been saved and spills a quarry's worth of poison into the valley.

## Beats

1. The siege camp at dusk: Cullen's plan, Cassandra's doubts and Bull's blunt reading of the walls.
2. A parley or a letter reaches Varn, and she answers in a voice that is half her own.
3. The gates fall, and the garrison, already changed, pours from the lyrium tunnels.
4. In the quarry's heart a huge, transformed templar guards the largest vein, and Varn watches from a ledge.
5. She must be spoken to, struck, or left; the quarry shudders either way.

## Choices

- Save Varn: she lives, but is marked by the lyrium and stands trial; Cullen is relieved and the army is wary.
- Kill Varn: the garrison breaks, the quarry is secured and Cullen carries it silently.
- Leave her to the lyrium: the army withdraws as the quarry collapses; Varn's fate stays open, and the lyrium's reach continues.

## Encounters

a2-quarry-gates, a2-quarry-heart.

## Outcomes

Redcrag falls or is sealed. Varn's fate is recorded and carried into act three: saved, she may speak at Tharsis's door; killed, Cullen's confidence wavers but holds; left, rumours of a crystal-fed captain follow the army west. Unlocks a3-staging-camps, a3-turned-agent, a3-seeker-archive and a3-merchant-princes. War-table starve-the-quarry may shorten the siege if committed.

## Low-energy version

Play it as the last night in camp before the assault: Cullen, Cassandra and Bull at the map table, Varn's reply on the desk, and the Inquisitor deciding her fate. The fight is a morning dispatch.
