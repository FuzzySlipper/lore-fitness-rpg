## Summary

A mercenary company led by Iron Bull, working for the Inquisition on the Storm Coast and beyond.

## Details

The Chargers are a tight-knit band of fighters, a mix of humans, elves, dwarves and others, who mock each other freely and trust each other completely. They fight smart and dirty, and they enjoy being paid. They keep a camp on the Storm Coast and a rowdy table in Skyhold's tavern. Krem, Bull's second, keeps the company's accounts and its manners.

They are loyal to Bull first, coin second, and the Inquisition third; the Inquisitor earns respect by treating them as people. Rest and good food are as much a part of their code as drilling. They know only what Bull tells them.
