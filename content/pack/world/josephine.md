## Summary

Antivan noblewoman and the Inquisition's ambassador and diplomat, one of its three advisors. She runs the war table's diplomatic and supply-minded projects from an office off the great hall.

## Details

Warm, quick and formidably organised, Josephine treats correspondence as a battlefield and a well-placed compliment as a siege engine. She came from a family of merchants and minor nobles whose fortunes are thinner than their name suggests, and she knows exactly what a favour costs. She keeps a writing board in one hand and a schedule in her head, and she remembers every petitioner's name. She advises on audits, relief, feasts and quiet letters to foreign courts, and she tends to find the polite path through a problem before anyone else has noticed it is blocked.

She notices when the Inquisitor has been training or travelling and makes it practical: a rested envoy is a better one, and she will reschedule an audience without apology. She never scolds a missed day; she moves the calendar.

She knows the Inquisition's ledgers are being tampered with, and she trusts Hadley Brisk's books more than her own tidy columns. She knows nothing of the Venatori's inner circle.

## Scene hooks

- She asks the Inquisitor to sit in on a negotiation, simply to be seen.
