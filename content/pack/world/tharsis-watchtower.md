## Summary

A ruined Tevinter watchtower in the Western Approach, Magister Tharsis's base in the third act.

## Details

A tall tower of dark stone leans above a ring of broken walls. Sand drifts in through its gates, and wind howls in its empty windows. The Venatori have repaired the stairs, hung banners and carved ritual circles into the floor. Cages and crates fill the lower halls, and the air smells of ash. At the top, Tharsis keeps a neat study with maps, letters and fine glassware. Whispers press at the edges of every room.

## When to use

At this story point the tower is where Tharsis has retreated, cut off from his supplies and gambling on a demon-backed strike at the Inquisition's camps. Guards are alert but stretched thin. Reaching the top means fighting through gate, hall and stair.

## Scene hooks

- A summoned demon strains at its circle in the lower hall.
- Tharsis's study contains letters that name his backers.
- A frightened servant offers to show a hidden stair.
