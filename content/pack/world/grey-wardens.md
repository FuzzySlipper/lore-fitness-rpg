## Summary

An ancient order sworn to fight the Blight, now silent and gathering in the Western Approach.

## Details

The Wardens are soldiers who drink darkspawn blood to fight the Blight, and they have long operated outside the usual politics of Thedas. At this point they have gone quiet, and rumours say that the Wardens are gathering at Adamant Fortress in the Western Approach, with no messages explaining why. Some fear they have been compromised; others hope they are preparing to help. The Inquisition has no clear answers yet.

Blackwall presents himself as a Warden, and others in the Inquisition regard that with hope. The march on Adamant is for season two; for now, treat the Wardens as a question that frames the Western Approach.

## Scene hooks

- A courier from a Warden outpost never arrives.
- An old soldier says the Wardens used to answer every call.
