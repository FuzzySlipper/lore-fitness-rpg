## Summary

Snow-bound mountain roads around Skyhold, the only supply lines through the range and the route of a lyrium smuggling ring.

## Details

Narrow trails climb between sheer rock walls and frozen streams. Caravans rely on a few chokepoints and hidden stone shelters. Avalanches block the roads without warning, and mules slide on ice. The air is thin and the nights brutal. Locals know small trails the Inquisition's maps do not; bandits know them better. Lanterns glow at wayside huts, where travellers trade news and share fires.

## When to use

At this story point winter is closing in and supplies are thin. Caravans are late and the Inquisition is watching every shipment, because tampered ledgers suggest red lyrium is moving along these roads. Scouts report unmarked wagons. The passes are tense but still passable if patrols keep them open.

## Scene hooks

- A caravan driver reports a sealed crate that was never on the manifest.
- An avalanche traps a supply train behind a narrow bend.
- Scouts find fresh wheel ruts leading off the main road.
