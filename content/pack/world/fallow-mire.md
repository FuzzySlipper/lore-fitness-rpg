## Summary

A drowned, fog-heavy Fereldan marsh with a long haunted past, now the site of the Inquisition's missing scouts.

## Details

Black water pools between rotting boardwalks and half-sunk huts. The fog hangs low and the ground sucks at boots. The silence is heavy and the dead are said to walk here. A weary, hollow dread presses on those who stay, and sound carries oddly. Old ruins and shrines emerge from the water. Fishermen and refugees avoid it. The air is damp, cold and slow.

## When to use

At this story point the Mire is a place the Inquisition would rather leave alone, but scouts have gone in and not come back. Despair demons and shades are rumoured. The Wardens and Corypheus's servants are not obviously here; the dread is older and quieter. Fatigue, not malice, is the first enemy.

## Scene hooks

- A scout's lantern is found floating, still lit.
- A trapper warns that the path shifts when nobody is watching it.
- The party must decide whether to rest in a dry ruin or push on through the night.
