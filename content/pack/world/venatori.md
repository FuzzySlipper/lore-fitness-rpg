## Summary

A Tevinter cult of mages and soldiers who serve Corypheus, funding and fighting his war.

## Details

The Venatori are ambitious Tevinter mages, retainers and hired muscle who believe Corypheus will restore their empire. They work through couriers, smuggling rings and bought officials, and field gladiators and spellbinders in battle. Many are true believers; some are opportunists who joined for coin or status. They are organised, secretive and arrogant, and they underestimate the Inquisition's soldiers. Their cells report to magisters such as Aurelian Tharsis.

They are the Inquisition's main human enemy in season one. Prisoners talk of the Elder One with fear and awe. They do not know the Inquisition's internal secrets.
