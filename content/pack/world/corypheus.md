## Summary

The Inquisition's great enemy: an ancient Tevinter magister, now a darkspawn, who tore the Breach open and destroyed Haven. He is alive and undefeated, and stays off-stage in season one.

## Details

Commonly known: the Elder One was a magister of old Tevinter who walked into the Golden City and returned changed, a darkspawn thing that survives death. He commands a dragon, the Venatori cult and an army of Red Templars poisoned by red lyrium. Everyone knows he caused the Breach and the fall of Haven; the Inquisition knows he wants the Inquisitor's power, and that the Grey Wardens' silence has helped him.

He is never present in a season-one scene. He is felt through his servants: a Venatori letter, a red-lit mine, a prisoner repeating a title, a dream that will not fade. Servants speak of him with fear and awe, never as an equal. His voice, if ever heard, is cold and contemptuous.

The Inquisition does not know where he is, what he plans after Haven, or how to end him.

## Scene hooks

- A prisoner will say only that the Elder One is displeased.
- Venatori orders found on a body carry a sigil nobody at Skyhold recognises.
- The Inquisitor wakes from a dream of red light and a distant, patient voice.
