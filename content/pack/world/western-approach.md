## Summary

A harsh, sun-blasted desert of canyons and old ruins, where the Grey Wardens are gathering at Adamant Fortress.

## Details

Red rock, dry wind and bone-bright sky. Water is precious and the nights are cold. Ancient Tevinter ruins stand in the dunes and canyons, some of them still in use by the Venatori. Caravans and scouts move cautiously between oasis camps. Heat ripples above the sand, and sandstorms can hide a column of soldiers. The place makes small mistakes expensive.

## When to use

At this story point the Wardens have gone quiet and are gathering at Adamant, but the march on Adamant is for later; here the Inquisition builds staging camps and scouts the approaches. The Venatori press the desert's edges, and the camps must be supplied across hard country. Expect heat, thirst and careful logistics.

## Scene hooks

- A camp's water barrels arrive half empty.
- A scout reports a ruined watchtower where lights burn at night.
- A sandstorm forces a column to shelter in an ancient ruin.
