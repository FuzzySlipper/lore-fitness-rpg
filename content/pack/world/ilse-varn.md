## Summary

Once a templar of real faith, now a Red Templar knight-captain holding a quarry-fortress for Corypheus. Act two antagonist who may be saved, killed or left to the lyrium.

## Details

Varn served the Order in Orlais and believed in its purpose; when the Order shattered she followed those who promised her a cause again. The red lyrium in her veins has made her certain, strong and increasingly unreliable. She keeps her order intact, drills her people and speaks of duty as a thing the Inquisition has betrayed. In quiet moments the old templar surfaces: the habit of kneeling, the stubborn mercy toward her own.

She is formidable in the field and in argument, and she is not a cackling villain. She is the sort of person the player could, with effort, reach, if the lyrium has not gone too deep. Her fate depends on the Inquisitor's choices.

She knows the quarry's workings, her patron's shipments and who in her garrison still doubts. She does not know Tharsis's wider plans, and she has never met Corypheus.

- A messenger arrives under truce to offer terms nobody in Skyhold believes.

- A deserter from Redcrag speaks of her with fear and loyalty in equal measure.
