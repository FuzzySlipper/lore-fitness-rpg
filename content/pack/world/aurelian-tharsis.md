## Summary

Venatori magister and the season's antagonist. A polished, patient Tevinter who funds Corypheus's war through smuggling and treats the Inquisition as an accounting error.

## Details

Tharsis is courteous, unhurried and never raises his voice. He speaks of lives as columns of figures, of war as an investment and of the Inquisition as a temporary variance he will correct. He wears fine, travel-worn robes, prefers letters to meetings and speaks to his enemies as though they were slow clients. His power is real; his confidence is the weakness. He cannot imagine being bested by people he considers beneath his notice, and he reacts to setbacks by revising his sums rather than reconsidering his view.

He runs the red lyrium smuggling through the Frostback passes, pays Ilse Varn's quarry and later gambles on a demon-backed strike from a ruined watchtower. Until act three he is a name on a ledger and a voice in captured letters.

He knows the shipment routes, his own paymasters and the Venatori cells. He knows little of the Inquisition's inner workings beyond what spies tell him, and he does not know the Inquisitor personally.
