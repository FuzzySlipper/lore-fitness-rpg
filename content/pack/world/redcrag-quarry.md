## Summary

A former Orlesian marble quarry in the mountains bordering the Exalted Plains, now Ilse Varn's red lyrium quarry-fortress.

## Details

Terraced white stone cuts descend into a deep pit. Old cranes and ramps now serve as walls and gates. Red lyrium veins glow in the quarry floor, and the stone seems to hum. A single road winds up from the plains, watched by lookouts. Inside, Red Templars drill and drag carts of crystal toward hidden wagons. The marble is stained dark, and the whispering is louder close to the pit.

## When to use

At this story point Redcrag is Corypheus's supply source, working at full pace and well defended. Wagons leave at night for the Red Road. A siege is possible but costly; starving the quarry of food and water is slower and kinder to the Inquisition's own soldiers. The fortress is not yet breached.

## Scene hooks

- A deserter slips out through a drainage cut and begs for sanctuary.
- Lyrium hum in the pit makes the Inquisitor's allies uneasy.
- A parley flag rises over the gate at dawn.
