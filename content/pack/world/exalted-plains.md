## Summary

Orlesian grasslands and ruins scarred by civil war, the road along which red lyrium travels.

## Details

Wide, wind-swept plains give way to burned villages, abandoned battlefields and old elven ruins. Orlesian nobles' forces and rebels have both marched through, leaving ditches and broken fences. Roads are patrolled by suspicious soldiers and the odd mercenary band. A few Rifts shimmer over old fields. At night the plains are very dark and very quiet.

## When to use

At this story point the Orlesian court intrigue has simmered down but the countryside is unsettled. Red Templar convoys travel along the Red Road under false banners, and local lords are wary of the Inquisition's soldiers. Ruins hold traces of old elven history, and the mountains to the north hide the red lyrium quarry at Redcrag.

## Scene hooks

- A burned farmstead hides a hidden cache of red lyrium shards.
- A local lord offers hospitality, and an unspoken warning.
- A convoy marked with false banners is spotted on the horizon.
