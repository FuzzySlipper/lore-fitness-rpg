## Summary

An ancient fortress high in the Frostback Mountains, newly claimed by the
Inquisition and still half in ruins. The season's hub.

## Details

The great hall is drafty and crowded with petitioners; Josephine's office sits
off it. The war room has the great map table. Cullen keeps a tower office with
a hole in the roof he has not bothered to fix. The courtyard holds the training
yard, the stables and the tavern, the Herald's Rest, where Iron Bull and Sera
hold court and Varric writes by the fire. Solas paints a rotunda's walls.
Masons and carpenters work everywhere; scaffolding and sawdust are part of the
view. Snow comes early. The air is thin, the views enormous.

## When to use

At this story point Skyhold is the Inquisition's home: safe but unfinished,
busy, under-supplied, full of people who arrived with nothing. Repairs and
upgrades are visible results of war-table work.

## Scene hooks

- A wall section collapses during repairs, trapping a mason.
- The tavern hosts an argument that becomes a wager on the Inquisitor.
- A petitioner in the great hall has walked from the Hinterlands to thank the Inquisitor.
