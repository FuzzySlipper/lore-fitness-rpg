## Summary

Rolling Ferelden farmland and forest south of Skyhold, scarred by the mage-templar war. A busy first stop for relief work and recruiting.

## Details

Rivers cut between hills of wheat, orchards and old stone mills. Roads are crowded with carts, and the Crossroads is a camp town of tents, kitchens and notice boards. Bandits and stray wolves linger on the edges. A few Rifts still flicker, and shades drift out of them after dusk. The villages are tired but hopeful, and they remember who sent the bread.

## When to use

At this story point the open fighting between mages and templars has been suppressed, but the land is full of refugees, wounded and people with nowhere to go. Redcliffe is recovering, farms are half-planted, and the Inquisition's name is spoken with relief. Expect rumours of Venatori couriers on the roads and hopeful new recruits. Corypheus's war feels distant here but the fear is not.

## Scene hooks

- A refugee family offers their only cow as thanks for a delivered sack of grain.
- A Rift hums above a field, and farmers want it closed before the harvest.
- Venatori couriers are rumoured to pass through a quiet mill.
