## Summary

A rugged, rain-battered coast on the Waking Sea, home to smugglers, wreckers and storm-chasing cliffs.

## Details

The coastline is black stone, rolling grey surf and endless gusts. Narrow paths wind between waterlogged ruins, driftwood camps and sea caves. Seabirds scream above the waves. The air tastes of salt and wet iron. Bull's Chargers keep a camp nearby, mercenaries enjoying their work. Smugglers and wreckers use the coves, and half the locals will sell anything to anyone. The weather is the other opponent.

## When to use

At this story point the coast is a rough, lawless fringe: Venatori hire hands here, and Orlesian and Fereldan traders are nervous about the war. The Chargers are working for the Inquisition and mostly keeping to the arrangements. Nothing is yet resolved with the Wardens or the Red Templars here.

## Scene hooks

- A storm drives a smuggler's ship onto the rocks overnight.
- The Chargers argue over a payment dispute with local fishermen.
- A fisherwoman offers directions to a cove that nobody visits.
