## Summary

Former templars poisoned by red lyrium and bound to Corypheus's will, led in the field by officers like Ilse Varn.

## Details

When the Order broke, some templars took the red lyrium Corypheus offered and gained strength, fervour and obedience. The crystal grows through armour and flesh, and those most changed become horrors and behemoths, barely human. Some are still devout and still believe they serve a holy purpose. Others are in pain and cannot stop. They hold quarries, guard convoys and raid settlements.

They are the Inquisition's second major enemy this season. They remember the Order's discipline, and their orders come through Corypheus's will rather than any chantry. They hold no secrets of the Inquisition.
