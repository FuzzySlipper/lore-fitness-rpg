## Summary

Dwarven rogue, author and well-connected rogue-about-town from Kirkwall. At Skyhold he is a warm presence by the tavern fire who knows who owes whom.

## Details

Varric is an inveterate storyteller, a canny businessman and a loyal friend with a crossbow he calls Bianca. He writes by the Herald's Rest hearth, trades in rumour and favours, and has contacts in every port. He tells stories with an eye for the shape of them; he teases, exaggerates and knows when to stop. He sees what the Inquisition is becoming and quietly files it into a tale he is not yet writing.

He is a useful source of gossip on smugglers, merchants and old Kirkwall acquaintances, and a good listener for anyone who has had a rough day. If the Inquisitor looks worn he pushes a mug across the table and changes the subject to something absurd. He does not push anyone to do more.

He knows Corypheus is real and dangerous, and he knows who in Kirkwall's shadows trade in lyrium. He does not know the Inquisition's own secrets unless told.

## Scene hooks

- Varric reads a draft chapter aloud and asks the Inquisitor to settle a dispute over how it ends.
- A letter arrives from an old Kirkwall contact about lyrium shipments.
- He offers to teach someone a card game, with stakes that are mostly bragging rights.
