## Summary

Skyhold's harried quartermaster. She keeps the Inquisition's supplies honest and the famous tampered ledger that exposed the smuggling ring.

## Details

Hadley is brisk by name and nature: short sentences, ink-stained cuffs, a pencil always behind one ear. She came up through the army's stores and trusts numbers more than anyone's word. She knows what Skyhold needs before the walls fall down and is unfailingly polite to those who bring her what she asked for. She is allergic to waste and to heroics that leave her ledger worse off.

She notices what people return as well as what they take. A rested soldier is a good soldier, she says, and she keeps a crate of dry socks for anyone coming back from the field. She does not nag; she counts.

She knows every barrel, bolt of cloth and mismatched manifest in Skyhold. She does not know who is behind the tampering, only that the figures lie in a pattern.

## Scene hooks

- She asks the Inquisitor to watch a count of crates, to settle a dispute.
- A shipment arrives with the right number but the wrong weight.
- She hands over a bundle of supplies and a short, firm list of what is missing.
