## Summary

An ancient, green-lit forest in Orlais, the site of an old elven battlefield and a source of timber and local goodwill.

## Details

Enormous trees rise over mossy roots, and sunlight falls in green shafts through the canopy. Elven ruins and statues sit half-buried among the ferns. Dalish hunters keep quiet camps, and wolves and hawks move in the shadows. The air is cool and sweet, and the place holds a stillness that makes people lower their voices. Logging camps work at the edges, and the Dalish watch them carefully.

## When to use

At this story point the forest is peaceful but tense: Orlesian timber crews and elven clans share an uneasy peace, and the Inquisition seeks timber for Skyhold. The Venatori have not yet disturbed the old ruins. Careful diplomacy brings goodwill; heavy-handed requests do not.

## Scene hooks

- A Dalish scout asks what the Inquisition wants with the trees.
- A logging crew finds an old elven statue in the way of the saw.
- A quiet clearing proves safe to rest and share a meal.
